   “Hello there,” said the man.  
“Do you know—what time it is?”  

She paused... then replied:  
    “I think it’s around five o’clock.”  

“Well,” he said, “that’s strange—very strange.”  

The room was quiet. Too quiet.  
    ‘Is something wrong?’ she asked.  

“No... nothing’s wrong,” he muttered.  

But deep down, he knew: something wasn’t right.
